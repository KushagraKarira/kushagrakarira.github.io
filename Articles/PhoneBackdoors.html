Smartphone Backdors ( Access Bypassing )

It happened on November 28, that i get am email from a friend saying that the phone screen is dead, and just a few weeks ago my brother’s phone power button stopped working – so i will explain how i work my way around those problems and a bonus segment.



Phone Display not working

This one’s kinda tricky, as I’d probably switch to a new phone as my data is on various cloud.

But if i don’t have a spare phone, my first reaction is to turn on the bluetooth !!

( ADB can be used if you have it enabled )

there are a lot of audio devices you have paired with blueetooth and that can easily activate whatever device assistant i have and ask it to do certain task – including the accessibility services.

God hail those phones that have a seprate assistant button

And in those old keypad phones we even had an option to read device files through bluetooth.



Power button not working

I got my brother’s phone switched off and power button not working,

first reaction was to enter ADB Sideload or Fastboot,

I could happily enter the fastboot mode by keeping both keys pressed and then plugging in the charger.

After that a simple fastboot reboot had me in the homescreen.

Password forgotten

This happened on two different phones,

on first – i could simply go to google find phone and set another password.

On second ( GoG tablet ) - After entering the wrong password about 40 – 50 times it auto factory reset ( those ##### don’t have fastboot ! )
