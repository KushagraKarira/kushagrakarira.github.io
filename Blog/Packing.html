[ GUIDE ] Packing for Travel

This article covers the list for a 7 Day travel, i made another 30 Day travel list but it is under test.

This is from trial and error from my experience of going home from Ahmedabad, but with some modifications based on weather of destination i have taken this to 10+ cities; (+) Thinking back in my life, my parents but more our community had this thing where young children would be sent off to relatives place where we'd spend few days of summer with them this not only helped me prepare to eventually leave home, understand the necessities that have evolved into this blog while also helping us understand how other families went on with their day to day lives.
