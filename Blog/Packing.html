[ GUIDE ] Packing for Travel

This article covers the list for a 7 Day travel, i made another 30 Day travel list but it is under test.

This is from trial and error from my experience of going home from Ahmedabad, but with some modifications based on weather of destination i have taken this to 10+ cities

You may add or delete items based on your preference and choices

Note to myself : Make these amazon links

  • Backpack
    • Laptop
    • Earphones
    • Small Notebook and Pen
    • Compression Cube
      • 3x Tees
      • Jeans
      • Track Pants with zipper
      • 2 Socks
    • Multi tool
    • Spork
    • Piezo Lighter
    • USB Cable and Charger(s)
    • Power Bank
    • Other Cables
    • Eateries
      • Refillable Bottle
      • Energy bars/ Biscuits
  • Phone
  • Watch
  • Wallet
  • Keys
  • Optional / Weather dependent
    • Glasses
    • Wind Cheater / Windbreaker
    • Hoodie with zipper
    • Gloves
    • Carabiner



Notes :
1 : Keep wallet in a place which needs two separate actions to access like the inner pocket of my jacket to protect it.
2 : Always be aware of your surroundings
3 : Carry a Surgical or N95 mask, not just for COVID but for dust or smell too.
