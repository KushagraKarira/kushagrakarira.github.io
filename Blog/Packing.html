[ GUIDE ] Packing for Travel

Updated : January 2024 ( Adjusted with my year's predictions )

This is something that has worked for me, tested again and again in multiple cities with temperature ranging from 2 to 50 Celsius and this can extend to max 7 days. From 7 to 14 days the clothing needs to be adjusted and beyond that its just adapting and adding more items locally.

I'm also assuming that there is infinite budget, focus is on durability, reliability and repairability

You may add or delete items based on your preference and choices

  • Backpack
    • Earphones
    • Small Notebook and Pen
    • Compression Cube
      • Top
      • Bottoms
      • Socks
    • Multi tool
    • Ability to start fire
    • Ability to Charge
    • Backup Power
    • Something to eat and eat with and water
  • Phone
  • Watch
  • Wallet
  • Keys
  • Glasses
  • N95 Mask



Notes :
1 : Don't keep wallet in back pocket, either inside zipped jacket or front pocket. Maybe Switch phone and wallet pocket for added awareness.
2 : Mental Presence to Observe and Analyse your Surroundings
