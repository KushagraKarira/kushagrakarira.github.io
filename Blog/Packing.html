[ GUIDE ] Packing for Travel

Updated: November 2024 (and a SHTF Go-Bag)

This is not a done deal, its being upgraded, so far it has worked in 30+ cities and tempratures 2-50 degree C, not accounting for rain. This is more of a 'essentials'/ problem solver, and not sustainable by design

I'm also assuming that there is infinite budget, focus is on durability, reliability and repairability

You may add or delete items based on your preference and choices

  • 50L Backpack
    • Sunglasses
    • Multitool with pliers
    • Piezo Jet Lighter
    • Headlamp
    • Headwear//
    • Compression Cube
      • Top
      • Bottom
      • Socks & Gloves
    • Carabiners and Nylon Webbing
    • Petrolium Jelly and Cotton
    • Something to eat and eat with and water
    • Technology
      • Spare Phone with Sim
      • Handheld Radio (Optional) to 466MHz and 150MHz
      • Ability to Charge and Charging Cable
      • Backup Power
      • Spare Batteries
      • Adaptors/ Connectors
    • Medicine/ Toiletry
      • Found toileteries from Hotels
      • Tape
      • Cotton
      • Petrolium Jelly
      • Paracetamol
      • Amoxycilin
      • Aspirin
      • Ibuprofen

Just be aware of your surroundings and bring the multitool to belt if security is low, bring the radio to belt if in remote area with no cell
