Evolution of Gods


What do i mean by evolution of gods, is that initially at the dawn of civilization we used to worship the sun god, then we slowly moved onto things closer to us like mountains, thunder and tree
and not so long ago we ( Hinduism and Christianity ) started worshipping the humanoids
and as a self-proclaimed philosopher i see another change, a change the final step of the evolution of our gods which is worshipping living people (and mostly communities) of people around us. I am not pointing to a specific group, but groups potentially competing to become to the next gods.
They have a name, the unholy cannot speak of, whatever they do is right and in general the masses are seen trying to become one ( the holy journey ) by imitating the features of their gods.
This is not the worst, The gods are powerless and are just puppets of the real people in power, an antichrist if you believe it.
