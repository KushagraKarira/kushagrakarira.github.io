The God Machine

About :

This started as a ripoff of the 'Miracle Machine' by the Blind Guardian. Their tracks have been the motivation for my poems, so why not a blog about how a machine that can turn thoughts to products. Thus this blog started and by the result of the machine itself i found the other side and a change to see the machine's inner workings.

The God Machine, as the title now seems perfect as i take the opportunity to break down what god is and use that in the context of a machine to explain the idea to the audience.

This will be divided into with first principles or what i love to call the process of alchemy of the understanding as 'What it is', the deconstructing as 'How it works' and finally reconstructing as 'How to use it'. Hint : It's hidden in the plain sight in our daily lives and its so obvious that explaining it almost feels like feeding the snake its own tail by teaching it.

The Framework :

Starting to build the foundation, the 'Principles of Truth' as described in the book 'The Kybalion' which highlights these as the teachings of Hermes Trismegistus. These serve as the initial framework or the upcoming model and seems to support it for me.
  1. The Principle of Mentalism : "The All is Mind; the Universe is Mental."

  2. The Principle of Correspondence : "As above, so below; as below, so above.” This principle embodies the truth that there is always a correspondence between the laws and phenomena of the various planes of being and life.

  3. The Principle of Vibration : "Nothing rests; everything moves; everything vibrates."

  4. The Principle of Polarity : "Everything is dual; everything has poles; everything has its pair of opposites; like and unlike are the same; opposites are identical in nature, but different in degree; extremes meet; all truths are but half-truths; all paradoxes may be reconciled."

  5. The Principle of Rhythm : "Everything flows, out and in; everything has its tides; all things rise and fall; the pendulum-swing manifests in everything; the measure of the swing to the right is the measure of the swing to the left; rhythm compensates."

  6. The Principle of Cause and Effect : "Every cause has its effect; every effect has its cause; everything happens according to law; chance is but a name for law not recognized; there are many planes of causation, but nothing escapes the law."

  7. The Principle of Gender : "Gender is in everything; everything has its masculine and feminine principles; gender manifests on all planes."

  8. The lost Principle, CARE : "You get where your attention goes, think between love and ignorance"

What it is :

Starting off with the God Machine, imagine a machine; I'm calling it consciousness a self aware machine which has choice. And it chooses to exist so it creates a program, an environment where it can interact with itself like the people in our dreams, i may be dreaming about my cat protecting me from monsters, in the dream we are different but as the dreamer the cat and myself are the same identity. That super entity or consciousness is god by the definition of this article.

And this player in this game/ simulation can be called soul or a user. This user is different from the player which has a intellect and senses of its own. With the level of involvement of the soul to determine the quality of entropy of the played.

How it works :

Basically it starts with the consciousness creating the most optimal game and then creating multiple units from itself ( which it can create infinite ) but are limited by the game and the capability of consciousness; Quality of the player decreases with the increase in number.

Then the rules of the game kick in and allow the user to take mulptiple choices resulting in more harmony or chaos. Which logs the interactions and thus affect the future choices of the player.

How to use it :

I am still exploring the optimal way but the simplest way would be to change the player variables and thus changing the probability distribution which directly affects what the outcomes will be. Ans there needs to be action as in trials to get the results.

And from my understanding it can be as simple as making another person trust in something and taking action.

From the player's perspective the state of superposition ends with the collapse of the wave function which is by the intent of the user which is based on the past actions of the player.

This is obviously a very limited part, and still expanding as there are many things that you can connect the dots to explain. Such as the with the second principle and the branches of future, there are similar branches in the past based on who you ask the farther in past the more differences in the stories same with the future the further you go the more the possibilities and intents but as the intents merge the present merges and the illusion of time is created.
