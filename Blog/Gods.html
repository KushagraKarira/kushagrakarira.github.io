As above, so below; as below, so above; as within, so without.

God, a being with infinite capability who can do anything. Sounded facinating to me as a child. Soon i found that this being is not impossible, then we became friends. and slowly we learned about each other, we wern't that different after all.

Let me explain, the mahavakyas of the upnishads. 'You are that', 'I am absolute', and now the fact that they are the most misunderstood truths. A good analogy to explain it is imagine the atnam to be like a droplet of water in a ocean. Yes, you posess the code to the entire ocean, you are a part of the ocean but you are not the ocean.
