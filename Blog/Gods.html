The God Machine


I'm diverging from the idea of the old guy in the clouds looking at you with a checklist,

Into an idea that it is a machine, something universally accessible and that everyone, every single one of has the capability to turn on this machine and ask for whatever they want and somehow the energy manifests itself and your desires come true.

I assure you it's very simple, but you will want more and you will want to look at how the machine works - that is the mechanic or as refereed as a Buddha.

Machine is an analogy to explain the bigger truth, that is :

All is one, One is all

It is that you are the universe - not something separate from it. Look at it this way - what you think is your body was initially what your mother ate and then what you did. The milk, leaves, roots, animals... and the elements of life flows though it in water and air. You are the part of the original Big Bang, you are a part of the universe just as anything observable to you. - This is the All is One While the One, or the god that we think is looking through the clouds is a part of this creation ( what's a god without those who worship ) and it is looking through its creation through your eyes it is you, it is inside you pretending to be you.

Nothing Rests, Everything Flows

Implying that everything is in motion, from the planets, to the solar system, to your mind everything is in constant motion. Everything flows.

Everything is dual

Everything has its opposites.

Equivalent Exchange

This simply builds on the idea 'For every action, there is an opposite reaction' or the law of Karma that everything you do will come back to you. Good thoughts will come back to you, so will the bad ones.

Every effect has its cause

The Principle of Causality, 'Every effect has a cause. Every contingent being has a cause. Whatever is reduced from potency to act is reduced by something already in act.' And that every cause has its effect and every effect has its cause.
