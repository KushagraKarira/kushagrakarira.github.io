[ The Store run by Ghost ]

::: Nothing in real :::

This store is about an old store on the side of a busy street, nothing about it stood out than the legend that ran in the town,

According to the legend the the store is run by a ghost and that the owner used to be a really good person who was devoted to his work after his kid died in a road accident, some think it was a way for him to move away from the grief and pain but he never wasn't open on the topic of his family and shop was all that he had. It was an old electronics shop where he'd sell other things as well and he could be seen working on the TV from the glass door but one day he saw a child crossing the road and rushed to save him while getting him killed in the way.

But with some miracle, to a regular person who doesn't know the story the store is like any other store, it gets opened up in the morning and it gets cleaned by the afternoon, even the few customers that place their broken electronics in the store get them fixed and by the night the store gets closed every day.
