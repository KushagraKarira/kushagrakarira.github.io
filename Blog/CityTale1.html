[ The Store Run by Ghost ]

::: Nothing is real :::

For years, the residents of an unnamed town lived knowing something nobody would believe, an that on a busy street a particular electronics repair store is run by a ghost, not an easy conclusion for them to make but the store would open by the first rays of the sun and close by the sunset and while the store functioned completely with taking in broken electronics placed in front by the residents of the city and collect them the next day in the store, as nobody had seen the owner, nothing more than apparition of the old man.

The urban legend that floated said that the man opened the store after his wife died by some unknown disease and after suggestion from his relatives he had set up the store to keep his mind busy but not long after when in a foggy cold winter morning when he was opening his store he saw a child crossing the road all by himself when a school bus was coming and in an attempt trying to save a child he crashed into the bus and bled to death. It is said that he had been given a second chance in life.

When the sun set, the store would not light up and the shutter would go close after which strange noises could be heard coming from the store. Nobody dared to go inside, everyone knew that it was the ghost working inside and helping out wherever he can.

The people of the town were grateful and welcoming for the good man's spirit, and as a way to show their appreciation and tribute, they often brought gifts along with their broken electronics to leave in front of the store.

The locals relish the story of the kindhearted man who continues to haunt the store and devote himself to repairing electronics despite being in the afterlife. His story provides an excellent reminder to all generations of the power of kindness and devotion.
