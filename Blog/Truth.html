Book of Powers


Introduction

For those aware, 'The Book of Powers' was the name of my original blog more than 15 years ago. The idea was a external object or a directory like a book that reveals onto the readers the truth.

Coming to the present day, present moment. I think i am very close to something that can change everything. It may be the most guarded secret in the history of humanity.

knowing that, i trust in the absolute that it won't be misused without consequences and that it isn't a flip-switch.

I for the past year am trying to find the 'truth' that i have celebrated so much in my poems, the truth being the unchanging absolute and with just a few pages into my copy of the Bhagwat Gita (Song of the lord) and lord Krishna in 2.20 says
'The soul is neither born, nor does it ever die; nor having once existed, does it ever cease to be. The soul is without birth, eternal, immortal, and ageless. It is not destroyed when the body is destroyed.'
So, is the soul the truth ? but before answering that what is a soul ? and is there a single soul or many souls ? This led me to the further journey to Alchemy, yes the primitive Chemistry or the art of transmuting objects by the Hermes Trismegistus who learned this from the angels. The tria prima, soul, spirit and the body, And with mindcrashing cultural overwrites my conclusion was that the absolute truth is the sprit, the soul is a representation of the spirit and the body a shell to bind this spirit to the world. This is a bit mindcrash here - let me explain.
From Joseph Campbell 'Just think, Vishnu sleeps in the cosmic ocean, and the lotus of the universe grows from his navel. On the lotus sits Brahma, the creator. Brahma opens his eyes, and a world comes into being, governed by an Indra. Brahma closes his eyes, and a world goes out of being. The life of a Brahma is 432,000 years. When he dies, the lotus goes back, and another lotus is formed, and another Brahma. Then think of the galaxies beyond galaxies in infinite space, each a lotus, with a Brahma sitting on it, opening his eyes, closing his eyes.'

and a mindcrash ahead, my understanding is that the Bhraman is like a person dreaming where every single person in the dreamer is the same thing the part of the absolute. and when someone asked me if there's outside this Bhraman - we may never know, it is the hermatic seal that cannot be broken it is like the players in a computer asking what is outside the room the computer is in. We may find another computer if this is a server farm but how will we know - i don't know and i don't wish to. I don't wish to know how the hen makes golden eggs but how to use the golden eggs and avoid taxes.

Coming to that, you many make the simulation aware of your existence and pretend to add another layer of self similarity.
I am still at this step of understanding the exact architecture of existence, and how does the 'As above, so below' and the prayer works. I am the player in a game trying to read the source code and edit it.
