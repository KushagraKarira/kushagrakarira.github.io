    Think you’re out of Diwali Quotes and Greeting messages ? Well, here we are to rescue

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

    Autumn has arrived, ringing the bells.Diwali is on its way my friends. I just love the concept of Diwali, everything, the season, chilly winds, home full of fragrance and sweets and the whole drastic change in the environment. I just don’t like to go to sleep, chatting late nights in the comfort of bed and blanket while eating sweets and bass is high enough to make me shiver feels so mesmerising..the only thing that tickles is googling over for quotes, greets, jokes and messages of such sort.

    So this time I decided to reposit most of those good ones at a single place, here.

    Quotes :

    “Deep Jalte Jagmagate rahe,

    Aap humko Hum aapko Yaad aate rahe,

    Jab tak Zindagi hai, Dua hai

    Aap chand ki Tarah Jagmagate Rahe.

    Happy Diwali”

    “Today’s the Festival of Lights all o’er;

    A joyful day for minds and hearts and souls;

    Laughter and smiles for many days;

    Let there be triumph in every way.”

    “The significance of Deepavali is the removal of darkness and ignorance from the mind and filing it with goodness.”

    — Sri Chandrasekhranda Saraswathi

     

    Greeting Messages:

    Candles of hope are burning bright, filling our hearts with eternal delight, hope,
joy, prosperity, and health.Wishing you happiness & peace.
 Happy Diwali.



A true and caring relation doesn't have to speak loud,
 a soft sms is just enough to express the heartiest feelings.
 Enjoy the festival of Diwali with lots of fun.




Lakshmi ka Hath ho, Saraswati ka Sath ho, Ganesh ka niwas ho,
aur Maa Durga ke Aashirwad se Apke Jeevan me Prakash ho.
Happy Diwali and New Year that leads you on to the road
of Success. A Bliss from Inside.



It Is Time To Feel Good,
 Time 4 Reunion,
 Time 2 Share Happiness,
 Time To Feel Being Loved,
 Time 2 Show Ur Love,
 Time 2 Live For Others And
 Time To Wish For Peace.
 HAPPY DIWALI. Cheers!!




Ek Duaa Mangte hai hum apne Bhagwan se 
Chahte hai Aapki Khushi Pure imaan se, 
Sab Hasratein Puri Ho Aapki,
Aur Aap Muskaraye Dil-o-Jaan se!!
Wish you a Happy Deepawali.





    "DIWALI" AS "ALI",
    "RAMZAN" AS "RAM",
    TELL ME THE DIFFERNCE,
    WHO IS HINDU WHO IS MUSALMAN.




May the festival of lights be the harbinger of joy and prosperity.
As the holy occasion of Diwali is here 
and the atmosphere is filled with the spirit of mirth and love,
here's hoping this festival of beauty brings your way,
bright sparkles of contentment, that stay with you through the days ahead.
    Wish you and Your Family a Very Happy Diwali.
    

For this, is a special time when family
And friends get together,for fun.
Wishing laughter and fun to cheer your days,
In this festive season of diwali and always!
Happy Deepavali
    

     
    

    

    May this Diwali bring you the utmost in peace and prosperity.
May the spirit of light illuminate the world.
May this Diwali bring joy, health and wealth to you.
May peace transcend the earth.
May this Diwali bring in u the most brightest and choicest happiness and love you have ever Wished for.
May This Diwali be as bright as ever.
May lights triumph over darkness. 
May the light that we celebrate at Diwali show us the way 
and lead us together on the path of peace and social harmony
May the festival of lights brighten up you and your near and dear ones lives.
Wish you and Your Family a Very Happy Diwali.






    

    

    

    Troubles as light as Air,

    Love as deep as Ocean,
    

    Friends as Solid as Diamonds,
    

    and Success as bright as Gold . . .
    

    These are the wishes for you and your family on the eve of Diwali.
