    Latest trending mehndi designs for Diwali 2015

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

     

    Since childhood, whenever I saw my mom or someone else scribbling designs on their hands with “Mehndi”, it gave me a shot of Awe and I drooled with wonder and the fact they used to say the longer you apply the darker it gets shivered me to heavens such a magical alike thing this mehndi is to me.

    Mehndi designing on arms,palms and feet are exclusively eye-catchy and gives an edgy ethnic look. Usually, this is carried as a festive tradition to apply henna on Kali-chaudas or Naraka Chaturdashi along with blinging up home with rangoli and decorations.

    Personally I find smell of orignal Mehndi very attractive, women quite often worry about distinguishing real mehndi from chemicals. That’s a very easy to sort, Mehndi powders are available in many colors, Orignal mehndi is green in color.Any other mehndi like of dark brown color is fake chemicals.

    Applying mehndi is an art that needs tremendous amount of creativity and practice which is perfected by experience. Here below are mentioned some of the most beautiful mehndi designs across the web selected carefully with a lot of time and patience just to save your time.
