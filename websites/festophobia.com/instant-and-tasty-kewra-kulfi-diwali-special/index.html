    Instant and Tasty Kewra Kulfi Recipe, Diwali Special

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

    Diwali comes in autumn.As the winter is begins to fall, this is probably best time to live the summer nostalgia in India. I and my friends usually try to eat as much ice-creams as we can, even competitions and Kulfi fills it all with a great satisfaction. The creamy solid texture and mild sweetness when served on a banana leaf leaves me frozen, lost, admiring its never-ending beauty. It can also be served as a complimentary dessert along with other sweets like laddoo, Shrikhand, Jalebi, Rabri. So this diwali celebrate a great share of kulfi made by your very own passion from bottom up.

     

    INGREDIENTS:

    1. Full fat whole milk – 750 ml
    2. Unrefined cane Sugar – 3-4 tbspns or as required
    3. Cardamom powder
    4. Almonds, Pistachios, Cashew nuts, Raisins
    5. Mawa(evaporated Milk) ~ 80 gms or 3-4 tbspns
    6. Rice flour – 1.5 tbspns or Corn starch 1 tbspn
    7. Kewra water 1 tbspn

     

    Steps :

    1. Take a wide bottom pan and begin to heat milk at medium flame.Heat the milk for 20-25 mins, this will ensure milk is thickened.
    2. Kulfi will be as tasty as much the milk is made near to Rabri.
    3. Mix Rice flour in 3 tbspns  of milk very well. Corn starch could be used but rice flour gives a creamy texture
    4. Crumble and grate mawa very well and fine using grater, no large lumps could be allowed.
    5. Cut all the dry fruits into very fine pieces, use mortar if needed.
    6. In the reduced Thick milk add sugar and Rice flour milk and mix it till dissolved completely, Avoid formation of lumps, in case any lump forms break them
    7. Also scrape  cream layers from side walls of kadhai/utensil into the milk.
    8. Keep it stirring slowly until it thickens.
    9. Add mawa, dry fruits and cardamom powder into the reduced milk.
    10. Keep stirring to ensure the uniformity of mawa and dry fruits in the recipe.
    11. Turn the flame down, add Kewra water and a pinch of saffron.
    12. Let the mixture cool, pour the mixture into the moulds, glasses or anything you want into.
    13. Scrape any left milk solids from inside the pan into the moulds as well.
    14. Ensure the lids of mould covered or if glasses are used cover with aluminium foil.
    15. Refrigerate for 4-6 hours minimum.
    16. After settling, Take them out of mould busing a butter knife or keep them in lukewarm water or you could try your luck rubbing the mould with your hands as well, it’s fun.
    17. Cut or slice or put a classical kulfi stick into to serve.
    18. Serve instantly, could serve over banana leaves or other big leaves to add a pop of  traditional essence.
