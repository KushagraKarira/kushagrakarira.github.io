      by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

      araka Chaturdashi, the second day of Diwali, falls on fourteenth day of hindu lunar month (waning moon),hence the name Chaturdashi, this day is also called choti Diwali as it falls before Diwali day. It falls after Dhanteras, 13th day of diwali.According to the legend Demon Narakasura was killed by Lord Krishna and Kali on this day.This day is also called Kali Chaudas ( Kali meaning Dark or eternal and Chaudas ,the fourteenth day of hindu month Kartik).In some regions of India this day is meant to Worship MahaKali or Shakti, who killed demon Narakasur, This day conveys to abolish laziness and evil which create hell in our lives.
