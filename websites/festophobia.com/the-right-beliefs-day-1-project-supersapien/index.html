    The 5 True Beliefs : Day 1 – Project SuperSapien

    by Saransh Karira in Project SuperSapien 

    To succeed over doing anything, all you need is a right mindset.We all have a pre-defined belief system based on the environment we are raised in. These set of beliefs decide the course of our actions in any particular situation.

    Like this small everyday incident from my childhood. Back when I used to live at my parents home, the city was full of water bodies and green lush. So there was an abundance of wild animals, especially the small ones. So whenever one popped out, let's say a snake, anyone else in my locality just used to freak out and kill that little thing brutally and violently.  They'd be sent to madhouse if they did the same when a person showed up! These people used beliefs over logic, they even had an old Indian tale that a chameleon's breath could poison you to death. But then there was I, locality didn't had many children of my age, so I grew up reading, gardening and watching TV , moreover the science channels. All those snake catchers, tiger whisperers, explorers just used to put me in awe. I loved to poke things around. So while they used to kill these little guys, I used to catch them, see how they did what they did, with so many time spent on watching these snakes on T.V, I literallly gained a sense of differing the poisonous snakes with venomless ones! Just by a glimpse of their skulls/Heads. Result? Till the date I have petted almost every living thing bigger than insects that could be found around the locality. Insects too but not all. I had an amazing life defining experience with each of them.

    So, What are the Right-Beliefs?

    Well, My whole one past year was spent in finding a right belief system. This is no joke fellas. If they have to stick and define my rest of the life. The best ones must be found. One thing I learnt over this whole year was –

    "Each and every Belief will seem right and correct in the mind of its orignator the one who ideated it, based on his/her's past environment, facts and beliefs."

    So, how did I achieve the right one?

    I was ready to find, I crushed my pre-defined values, virtues, beliefs. I got lost. I stopped watching news and same kind to prevent any priming or bias. And each day I sticked with a new belief, tried it over the whole day and noted the experience. Also I discovered that

    "Each and every person is good and just in his own eyes."

    from affirmations like  “I don’t care, I’ll do whatever I will to”, “live in the moment” to even giving myself names and titles like “The smartest man alive”,  “The Alpha” and what not! and then in each situation I would just run it against the situation and came up with an appropriate action. I’ve tried almost everything, every belief, of theist and of atheists as well.

    Some worked really well, but their effect faded over time. Others had to be memorised to be working.

    I needed something better and I kept searching it.

    During this whole time I developed an eye to find the insecurities of people behind their ways to act, the roots of many people’s prides and egos.

    With every new belief, I lost some faith, because I was so variable, someone different every other day. I forgot my orignal thinking patterns how I used to act, I was completely self-absorbed.

    But the life, It is full of surprises.When you are thinking about something and suddenly its answer pops itself into you from many different places all at one time.

    This is what I found and In which I found myself.

     

    • LIKE IT? DO IT –

    Do not stress over the outcome, This is over thinking. It will put you into the habit of thinking about the future every time, minimising your actions. Reduce the temporal gap between thinking and action. Remember,

                                   “What will come later, will be dealt later.”

     

    • FOCUS:

    Guys, our brains could focus only on one single task at a time, multitasking is nothing but switching the focus again and again. So fall into the habit of doing only one thing at a time, with your whole self. you can’t even feel the fear if you are devoted because even fearing needs to switch the focus!

     

    • FEARS ARE SEXY- :

    The whole time we used to perceive fear as a negative entity. It is not. The fears are actually the best thing of being alive. Feeling the fear means our mind is notifying us “Psst! Look We haven’t tried this thing before, It will be new, thrilling and exciting.”, Go for it.

     

    • BELIEVE -:

    Believe that whatever you’ll do will be right. It doesn’t matter what you did, you’ll end up justifying your actions. RESULT? Well, you’ll do anything to prove yourself that you did the right thing, so if that is the goal, than believe in it without justification, before even acting.

    • ENJOY -:

    Above all the things mentioned above, the single most important thing to remember while doing anything is to ENJOY DOING IT!! That’s it. Forget everything, just enjoy!  Irrespective of other’s presence or pleasing them in any way, or to prevent your glass ego or pride from shattering, keep everything aside and just Enjoy. This may sound very simple and nothing new, but the thing is many have forgotten just to enjoy themselves, their grace and yes it is perfectly effective.

     

     

    Sticking hardcore to these 5 power-plugs,  have changed my life forever, I do not need to depend over any motivation or external stimuli for any task. I just do.
