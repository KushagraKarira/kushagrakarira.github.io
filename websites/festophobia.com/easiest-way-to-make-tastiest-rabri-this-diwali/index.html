    Easiest way to make Tastiest Rabri this Diwali

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

    I vote Rabri as the queen of all sweets, the way it rolls over you tongue is just so refreshing. You could literally forget all your worries and could dive into the pleasure it gives. And as all the best things goes by it too needs a lot of patience. Let’s not give our patience here but rather making it.

    For making Rabri you’ll need a wide flat bottom vessel called Kadhai.To ensure that milk evaporates quickly.

    Ingredients:

    1. Whole fat Milk – 1 ltr.
    2. Sugar – 2 Tbspns or add by taste.
    3. Flavoring – Saffron and Cardamom
    4. Garnishing – Almonds,Pistachios and Resins
    5. Rose water/Kewra water(Pandanu water) – 1 Tbspn.

     Procedure:

    1. Put kadhai on simmered gas and pour milk into it.
    2. Keep it as it is, initially froth and then a layer of cream will begin to form.
    3. Now with a spatula take this layer sideways. stick it to the side walls of kadhai.
    4. Wait to let another layer form, now again stick this to the side walls.
    5. Stir the milk slowly so that it doesn’t gets burnt from bottom. Fast stirring will scrape the milk solids off the walls.
    6. Repeat the process over and over again. Scraping, Sticking and Stirring – The 3 S.
    7. After some time, The milk will be half of its original volume, You could pull over now or even go on up to reducing it to a quarter.
    8. At this stage add sugar, pinch of saffron strands and powdered cardamom.
    9. Stir lightly and make sure sugar gets dissolved completely.
    10. Scrape off the stuck milk solids from side-walls into the milk.
    11. Peel of the skin of Almonds, and Soak them along with pistachios and resins in hot water. This ensures that dry fruits aren’t dry enough to ruin the texture that comes in mouth.
    12. Crush them into smaller pieces and add them into our Rabri.
    13. Add Kewra water or rose water into the milk and stir slowly.
    14. Mix the milk lightly but well enough.
    15. Pour this into a bowl and put it into refrigerator for 3-4 hours.
    16. The Rabri is ready to be enjoyed over.
