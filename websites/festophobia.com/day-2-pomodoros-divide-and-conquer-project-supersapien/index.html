    Pomodoros – Divide and conquer – Project SuperSapien

    by Saransh Karira in Project SuperSapien 

    We all distract from our tasks several times a day, lose focus and get lost. This is perfectly normal, the higher brain gets tired early on and then the primitive brain that is the reptilian brain takes over. The primitive brain thinks about primary needs and instincts for survival. This includes Food, Sleep and Sex. This switching has a great impact on our productivity during the entire day. It is greatly reduced.

    How to Overcome this?

    While the switching is not in our control. We could certainly prevent the exhaustion which in turn will keep the higher brain in control. To acheive this we will use the Upgraded Pomodoro Technique!

    What is Pomodoro Technique?

    “Pomodoro” is the italian word for Tomato. The Pomodoro Technique is a time-management technique. In this the big tasks are divided into short time intervals. Usually 25 minutes, called  “pomodoros”. followed by a break. This regular intervals ensure that the brain stays refreshed to its optimum levels throughout the task.

    The Technique itself is divided into six gradual steps.

    1. Decide the Task needed to be completed.
    2. Set the timer to 25 minutes (can be varied) or 1 pomodoro.
    3. Work on the task in hand until the timer rings, If you get distracted by any thoughts write them down on a paper and immediately return to the task.
    4. For every pomodoro completed put a check mark on a paper.
    5. If Pomodoro checks are less than or equal to 4 check marks, take a short 5 minutes break after each pomodoro.
    6. If checks exceed number 4 then take a longer break 15-30 minutes.

    The planning part is essential. It gives a sense of how much time each task needs.

    The technique is an effective tool to minimise interruptions on flow and focus, as you cannot divide a pomodoro further. every distraction is either noted and postponed or the pomodoro is cancelled if the task is of high-priority.

    Ticking off check-marks give a sense of completion and accompalishment as the marks increase.

    Visualisation part is important. If you think you can mentally divide the clock time into pomodoros and work, then no. It won’t be very much effective. You have to put set the timer to create that mental sense of urgency. same goes for ticking and writing  distractions with paper and pencil. Trust me, when I say this. I have committed those mistakes. Learn from my mistakes.

    So,  how do we use this technique to create a productive day?

    The idea behind is, first of create a To-Do list for the day. Now divide the whole day into several pomodoros. Basically, what you have to do is start thinking about the day in terms of pomodoros, 25 minutes are not necessary. Like 8-10 pomodoros to sleep, 4 pomodoros to read, 1 pomodoro to wash clothes etc. Since the To-Do list is created early in the day when the higher brain was in its optimum level. You need not to worry about what to do next. Working under Pomodoro will keep you under sense of urgency. While the breaks at the same time provides time to assimilate and refresh.

    I have tried the basic technique already and it is damn effective. I am currently tracking my Upgraded version of the technique. So far, It is Damn effective. I will post the results of over the course of next 7 days.
