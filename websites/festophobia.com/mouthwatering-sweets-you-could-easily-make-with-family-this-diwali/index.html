    Mouthwatering sweets you could easily make with family this Diwali

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

    Ever since I was born, I am a  hungry devil lurking out to pleasure my tastebuds, and festival of lights ladies and gentlemen, is a 5 day food festival to me, I eat and eat and eat more and never get bored, all those plethora of sweets and salty snacks to compensate them. The sweets are one hell of reasons I thank god for dropping me as an Indian, just a mention of their name and my mouth becomes glacier of saliva. And the variety!! Rabri is my favorite!

    So I researched over and found out that many of them are pretty exciting to make by ourselves. I have made variety of desserts before but Indian sweets! never before… So this Diwali I am gonna make my whole family sit together and we’re gonna enjoy some quality time having fun around making them together.

    I researched all the recipes over various sources and have added my own experience into as well.

    Don’t just read, Enjoy trying!

    “You don’t have to live long,

    you just have to live”

    These sweets are called “Mithais”.The key ingredients are Thick Condensed milk or yoghurt, rice flour, Chickpea flour, various lentils and grains, squashes, dry nuts like walnuts, cashew-nuts, pistachio, almonds, chirongi along with resins are used, often. Cloves, Nutmeg, Cinnamon, Cardamom are preferred for fragrance. While muffled up with saffron, Silver leaf, Rose water or Kewra (Pandan Leaf) water.

     

    So here begins the Treasures of Yum-Yum,

    1.  Rabri (Yeah, i love to discriminate!)
    2.  Mawa Kachori/Kachodi/Kachaudi/Kachauri (dialects people dialects, fun to yell them over and over inside your head)
    3. Jalebi
    4. Kewda Kulfi (Ice cream)
    5. Laddoos
    6. Mishri Mawa
    7. Barfi
    8. Sohan papdi
    9. Shrikhand
