    ShriKhand : The simpler way, New flavours, Great Diwali Sweet food

    by Saransh Karira in Diwali 2015 

    Shrikhand is basically a Yoghurt based ice-cream but much tastier, healthy, fast and easy to make.It originated from Gujarat to Maharashtra to everywhere else. Diwali comes in autumn, which is the best time to enjoy it. Most people find it hard to figure out the right way to make it best. It’s quite simple once you get the base structure. Commercial Shrikhand comes in two flavoured varieties : Kesar-Elaichi & Mango flavoured shrikhand but I have tried Strawberry, and Chocolate myself and they are slurpilecious..This is usually eaten after dinner along with other sweets such as Rabri, Laddoo. Kulfi, Jalebi etc.

    Ingredients:-

    1. Curd/Yoghurt – 1 ltr
    2. Sugar or jaggery or honey according to sweetness
    3. Saffron
    4. Cardamom

     

    Procedure:-

    1. Take a screener and cover it with a muslin/cotton cloth.
    2. Put this screener over a bowl.
    3. Now pour all the yoghurt on this muslin cloth.
    4. The yoghurt must be fresh, sour one will make Shrikhand sour as well.
    5. let it settle, till now the whey would have started dripping into the bowl through the screener.
    6. Take all four corners of cloth and wrap one around other three and make a knot.
    7. Now press firmly downwards, our motive is to separate all the whey.
    8. Do not force it too much or the yoghurt will make its way through holes as well.
    9. Now take this apparatus into refrigerator and either put something heavy over the cloth or hang this cloth, the bowl underneath must be deep enough so that the whey filled into doesn’t reaches to the screener or else separation will stop.
    10. Keep it as it is from 8-24 hours.The longer the better but only as long so it stays fresh.
    11. After this time completes, take out apparatus, save the nutritious whey for other wide uses.
    12. Open the cloth, Now you have solid mass of what is called “Chakka”.
    13. This Chakka is what you could say raw “Shrikhand”.
    14. The Chakka is the base of our Shrikhand, It gives the texture now you could add your desired flavor.
    15. For Kesar-Elaichi add a pinch of saffron into half tbspn of hot milk and keep it aside.
    16. Now crush green Cardamom into powder using mortar and keep it aside as well.
    17. Now add your desired sweetener according to your taste into the Chakka
    18. Add saffroned milk and cardamom powder over and mix well, you could use an electric beater or a blender.
    19. Mix until it thickens and everything mixes well and sugar gets dissolved into.
    20. Refrigerate to settle.
    21. Add dry-fruits,nuts and resins to garnish.
    22. fresh-fruits like grapes, pomegranate could be sprinkled as well.
    23. This Shrikhand could now serve 4-5 people.
    • To make mango,strawberry or other fruity flavor shrikhand, just add the desired pulp.
    • To make Chocolate flavored Shrikhand add 2 tablespoons of cocoa powder and garnish with Choco-chips/buttons.
