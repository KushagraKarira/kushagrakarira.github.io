    Declutter : The art of reducing noise – Day 3 |Project Supersapien

    by Saransh Karira in Project SuperSapien 

    For any project to be efficient as well as successful, the building base and blocks must be focused upon from the beginning. As we all know during the course of the day our mental strength exhausts gradually.We need to completely focus all our mind power and focus on the task in hand and without wasting any of it in unnecessary distractions and tasks. For this necessity our mind should be decluttered. declutter means to reduce the noise.The noise is anything unnecessary for the task that comes up in mind. This could be mental as well as physical. To begin the project the base should be focused on simplicity.

    Simplicity is the key to reduce the noise.

    You too may have observed how simple the great people live. Benjamin Franklin, despite being relatively wealthy, lived a very simple life. This enabled him to invest his quality time over more important things, thus ended up inventing stoves, discovering electricity and even founding a country.

    Things require maintainance, the less you accumulate the lesser you need to maintain. A man is not characterised by his possessions but by his deeds. There are certainly many solid reasons to Declutter.

    • Decluttering reduces stress. Ever felt that feeling after cleaning up you messy room? Like a clutter from your mind has been cleaned as well.
    • It is the best way for a fresh beginning. To move out from the old rot back into that neutral zone. It will significantly kick-start your life. Holding onto stuff that’s associated with bad memories and feelings will keep the negative vibes flowing again and again into your system. Decluttering will give you a fresh, white canvas to begin with.
    • Saves your time. You’ll spend a lot more time finding the required stuff amongst your mess than in  a well organised room.

    The big question.

    How to Declutter?

    Physical space :

    • Assign enough pomodoros–

    Yes, we are now gonna talk in the terms of pomodoros. The number of pomodoros you’ll need will depend on the size of your house and amount of crap it holds. Usually it takes me 3 pomodoros to completely organise from the junkyard of crap.

    • Divide and Conquer –

    Divide the task into various smaller chunks. Like a house into rooms. Rooms into clothes, eatables, bed etc. Then focus and finish one chunk at a time.

    • Attack with the right mindset –

    Apply the right filters on your mind. People usually try to keep everything. Some were never used, they can’t let them go waste. Others are unneccessary but holds memories. All I will say is Learn to let go and watch how untangled your life becomes. The mindset should be like – Is this useful? If yes, do I use it regularly? Last time I used it? When again will I use it in near future?

    • Keep the balance –

    Basically, get rid of something every time you bring home something new, This rule of thumb will prevent clutter from re-entering your life.

    Life :

    • Begin your day with meditation. It will give you enormous amount of mental focus to tackle throughout the day.
    • Wear simple and comfortable clothes. Ever wondered why successful people nowadays have begun to wear same outfit everyday? Steve Jobs wore a black mock turtleneck, blue jeans, New Balance sneakers and kept short hairs . A lot of mental energy in required to take a decision, and task as unnecessary as wearing clothes or a hairstyle do not worth that much of energy that too so early during the day. Also it saves you from a lot of worries like how do I look?
    • End your day with a journal and a To-Do list for the next day. This will ensure that you go to sleep decluttered after putting out all your worries of past into a journal and future into To-Do list.

    The effects of decluttering will be tracked everyday and results will be posted time to time.
