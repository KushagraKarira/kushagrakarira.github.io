So there was an abundance of wild animals, especially the small ones. So whenever one popped out, let's say a snake, anyone else in my locality just used to freak out and kill that little thing brutally and violently.  They'd be sent to madhouse if they did the same when a person showed up! These people used beliefs over logic, they even had an old Indian tale that a chameleon's breath could poison you to death. But then there was I, locality didn't had many children of my age, so I grew up reading, gardening and watching TV , moreover the science channels. All those snake catchers, tiger whisperers, explorers just used to put me in awe. I loved to poke things around. So while they used to kill these little guys, I used to catch them, see how they did what they did, with so many time spent on watching these snakes on T.V, I literallly gained a sense of differing the poisonous snakes with venomless ones! Just by a glimpse of their skulls/Heads. Result? Till the date I have petted almost every living thing bigger than insects that could be found around the locality. Insects too but not all. I had an amazing life defining experience with each of them.
