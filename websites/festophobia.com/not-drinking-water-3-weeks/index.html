    This is what happens when you drink no Water for 3 weeks and suddenly 6 Litres for next three

    by Saransh Karira in Project SuperSapien 

    Often sometimes we live by passing through whole day when we suddenly realise that we haven’t drink any appreciable amount of water whole day or nothing at all. Have you ever tried to find the effects it can have? Well this student went to extremes to find its practical effects. Here is his experience…

    We all know how crucial water is for the very existence of the life on the planet. Our human bodies are said to be containing  around 60%  of its total mass as water. We can live more than a month without water but can’t stretch further than 3-4 days without drinking water. Our bodies are said to be dehydrated already when we feel thirsty.
    Still , after knowing all these facts how many of us really even drink the minimum recommended intake required for the optimum health?
    Many of us don’t, knowing that we are alive anyway becomes an excuse and consequently a habit. I used to be the same. Careless about, and even proud sometimes when I realised that I have forgotten to drink it even for once in the past day or two. The very fact that we can’t stretch without water for more that 3-4 days and I have lived without it for 2 days felt like an accomplishment, an achievement. Was it? Well, the fact stands when we are going through without water as well as skipping the meals i.e. ingesting nothing at all. Looks like our colon sucks the water out of digested meals in case we are not drinking enough fluids.
    Was it really okay to go with? Does it have any consequence at all?
    We’ll never actually know without a practical comparison. Guess what? I just did that.

    THE EXPERIMENT  –

    The purpose of this experiment was to compare the practical effects caused due to not drinking enough water and due to drink enough of it.

    For a clear comparison, I went to the possible extremities. What crime did I commit?

    I lived without water for 3 weeks and then drank 6-7 liters a day for the next 3 weeks

    Of course I ate my meals, a glass of milk and fruits and Of course I am taking them anyway because I need to do workouts everyday.

    To minimise any kind of placebos or disbelief, I tried not to judge myself and compare on early stages like from day one, Instead I journaled each and every day of these six weeks and on 43rd day I went through them and noted down all the differences observed.

    Week 4 to week 6 were most interesting to explore. Anyone would assume three weeks without water!!  He’s gonna drink oceans when they end. So did I.

    DAY 1 – In the morning of first day of week 4, I still remember clearly, I woke up and took a bottle of water in hand yelling out “FINALLY!!” but I opened the cap and didn’t yet feel any urge to drink, and wasn’t able to drink more than half a glass before I felt full.

    Looks like there is something which I termed as “Water-appetite” and this craving and capacity directly depends on previous drinking habits rather than how much we have already drank. Such beautifully our body adapts.

    I was determined, I made a cross table giving 8 columns for each day. Aiming at 8 litres a day and marking a tick off after each litre. Bought four 1 litre bottles and filled them full and marked them 1,2,3,4 respectively. The task was to drink a bottle a time in one go as soon as I touched one with lips, no matter how full I got, no matter how repulsive I felt. The only urge I focused while drinking was to finish it ASAP and take the pen in hand and mark the column. The bottles were filled twice a day. I drank 6 of them on DAY 1, and was already peeing about every half an hour. Woke up in the middle of sleep 5 times.

    DAY 2 – Thinking that the body will adapt eventually I decided to drink 6 bottles again. This time a miracle happened. My Water-Appetite was improved significantly in just one day. I craved for water, felt thirsty and emptied a bottle as soon as it touched the mouth without a second thought. Although the full stomach I felt after was regretful. The pee patterns were same but I drank 7 bottles that day.

    DAY 3 – I wanted to increase the peeing intervals now, but intake can’t be reduced, so I tried drinking it slowly, and half a bottle a time. And limited  the drinking to 6 bottles a day. The results were significant. After the noon the peeing reduced to once an hour, which was much more comfortable than last two days and woke up just once while sleeping.

    DAY 4 – DAY 21 – From day 4 it felt stabilized and manageable and is a routine till now, but I’ll consider only up to day 21.

     

    THE EFFECTS  –

    The Energy –

    During the first three weeks, I was getting tired easily during my workout sessions. I took many rests during the sessions. I felt tired all day long. The effects I noticed after the experiment on the overall energy were,

    • Increased Stamina – I could be persistent during workouts for longer intervals.
    • Reduced recovery time – The time I took for rest during workouts was decreased.
    • Body heat was regulating lesser during workouts – I began to appreciate sweating!
    • No Laziness/Fatigue – Remember that laziness inside the muscles that we feel often much more during mornings? It’s all gone.
    • Energized – Felt full of energy all day long didn’t get bored or down for a single moment.

     

    The Mind –

    I used to be distracted, completely lost, unable to focus, whole days were spent off thinking and dreaming, couldn’t read on a single topic for more than 15 minutes. What changes would’ve occurred could be guessed, but I never knew that all this time this was the reason behind!!

    • My thoughts were clearer – I could clearly get the idea of what I have to do next and the purpose it’ll serve.
    • Focusing on a single thing was a child’s play – I even completed a book of 400 pages in 5 hours straight, without a single break.
    • I emotionally felt refreshed and cheerful all the time.
    • No Mental Fatigue – There’s a thing called mental fatigue, since teenage hit me, I used to get mentally fatigued. Now what this simply meant was that after using the mind over the day for various purpose like reading, making decisions etc. the higher intellect switches off and we become unproductive until we go to sleep. This wasn’t the case anymore, I felt like I was back to my childhood.
    • Most importantly I was totally present in the moment and was living it.

    • Body Wastes –

    • Satisfaction – In the earlier 3 weeks I was literally shitting stones and It hurt. Nowadays I go in and I am out the very next minute. I just feel so much lighter and weightless, you know that burden-less feeling. Its heavens!!
    • Pee colour – First three weeks I peed less and it was yellow and had odour, Now Its as clear as the water I drink ( I ain’t drinking that! 😛 ) and is not smelling anymore.

    I feel it was a bit extreme of an experiment, but yes you have to go to the limits to find the real answer. The experience was moreover an exploration and I enjoyed it all through. Now, finally we know some really interesting effects of not so interesting looking water. As they say, Simplicity is the ultimate form of sophistication.

    Currently I am doing 3 experiments side by side and one is a long-term. Of course I’ll share the ripe fruits as always.
