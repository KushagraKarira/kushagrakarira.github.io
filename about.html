I am right here, right how - A1

I failed to stick to a single identity and thus i am all, i am who or what you want i am who and what you see i am. I can be the master of masters or the poorest of poor, i can be your best friend or worst enemy, who do you want me to be ? If i were to choose, i am you.

About the website, it has a total size of less than 1MB and a appimage to run it offline which is around 80MB. The cost of retaining the domain and maintaining the other functions is offset by revenues from Pling. Future plans include adding pay-to-use services like VNC, SSH, DNS that are run off a satellite internet service and powered by solar.
