Self Conflict

I love you, i hate you
i am you
to the greatest depth of your thoughts
i am you

what have you done,
telling me who to love who to hate
planning my future
so i don't get to choose anything

yes i am you
from your deepest fear to your happiest moment
i know you better than you know yourself
tell me what you need

till my earliest memories i don't know who you are
calling yourself a part of me, shameful
telling me that you know the truth
telling me you can, i am tired of you

rhyming to the music i listen,
criminalizing me for your stupid poems
I'm done with you, go exchange me
for the memories of the one you die to meet

i didn't mean to, i didn't mean to make you cry
sorry
I'll listen to whatever you have to say
i just meant, let me take some decisions myself

i understand what you feel,
but you are mindless without me,
i am your mind afterall
now go, just don't come to me like a lost voyager

- Kushagra Karira
