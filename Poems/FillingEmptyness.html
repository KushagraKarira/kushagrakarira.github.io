Filling Emptyness


Day in, day out,
Listening to your voice,
The only thing i trust,
In this nightmare.

Whatever may come,
We stand together,
Sharing the pain in half,
Pleasure in double.

Calling your name,
when mine can't be heard,
going through memories,
only to cone back.

Laughing when you said,
We might not meet again,
Oh no, this is a crime,
You won't be forgiven,
Not even forgotten.

It's all empty now.

- Kushagra Karira

Another poem written for a my best friend, it was the last day of 10th grade when a joke we made for the last 4 years turned true it was the last day we'd see each other. I might not be well connected but there are moments in life when something unusual is going on, he knows and the memes find me.
