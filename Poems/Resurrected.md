# Resurrected

Another day begins,
another dream,
without your smile,
without your anger,

calling your name,
only echoes heard,
looking for you,
only memories found,

this is your friend,
following your steps,
falling to walk,
picking myself up,

just like you used to,
drag me to sunshine,
just like i used to,
hide my smile in your shadow

where have you gone
count to three,
I'll find you free,
no i can't see,

beyond life's reach,
beyond dreams,
in your nightmare,
I'll find you

Wait for me

- Kushagra Karira
