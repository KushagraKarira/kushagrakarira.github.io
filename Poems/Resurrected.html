Resurrected


Another day begins,
another dream,
without your smile,
without your anger,

calling your name,
only echoes heard,
looking for you,
only memories found,

this is your friend,
following your steps,
falling to walk,
picking myself up,

just like you used to,
drag me to sunshine,
just like i used to,
hide my smile in your shadow

where have you gone
count to three,
I'll find you free,
no i can't see,

beyond life's reach,
beyond dreams,
in your nightmare,
I'll find you
Wait for me

just like the time,
we used to share,
laughing and joking,
crying and fighting,

you were always there,
by my side,
now you're gone,
and i'm all alone,

i'll never forget,
the moments we shared,
the happiness,
the sadness,

the love,
the anger,
the friendship,
the fights,

i'll never forget you,
my friend,
my confidante,
my companion,

until we meet again,
someday,
somewhere,
in this lifetime,

or the next


- Kushagra Karira

This poem is dedicated to my friend, who took his life under stress.
He was my first friend when moving to MMPS, when my life was in constant chaos he was the one thing i counted on to be there every single day and help me, i took him for granted and did't realise that till i lost any possible chance to thank and to apologise. Remember this poem as a lesson to live every single day and that nothing can be taken for granted not even your own life. The title is another message to let the memories of people die, keep them alive in your memories.
