# Ragnarök's Mercy

Here come the words
i don't know will fade away
or remain forever as
the first part of eternity

back when the silver mage
forged the wheel of time
the destiny was written onto it
whoever lives must die,
and whoever dead must live
when the time resets the prophecy will begin,
one after another you will have the gifts
of the universe as it takes all away.

with each passing wheel
you will become what you are not
to become everything and
to fade away into nothingness
to create the law and escape from it,
to beg for love and cry for its creation
when nothing else is left
you will find yourself
the one that was lost
while looking for a purpose
an oath was taken to create
the world of dreams

the wheel of time belongs to none
but everyone wants to be a part of it
the wheel of time is unstoppable
and so is your will,
go forge the chariot for this wheel

- Kushagra Karira
