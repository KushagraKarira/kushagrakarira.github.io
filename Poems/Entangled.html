Entangled

Waking up to the chant
Waking up in your temple
I've lost myself

Is this the beginning of a life
Or the end of a dream
I cannot tell

Is the world a memory
or a fragment of human desire
that brought us together

the threads that were created
entangled forever
as the fabric of life

fold it, stretch it,
make it sad, make it happy
it is limitless and indestructible

the creator and the subject
brought together
a meaningless game
Wake me from this entanglement

- Kushagra Karira
