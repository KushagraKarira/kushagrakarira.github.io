Waking up to the chant of your name
Waking up in your temple
I've lost myself

Is this the beging of a life
or the end of a dream
i cannot tell

Is the world a memory
or a fragment of human desire
that brought us together

like threads of life
ours were carefully woven
to create this fabric of life

each time i wish for something to happen
there you are
holding my desires in the palm of your hands

like a goddess
each time i fall
there you are picking me up
