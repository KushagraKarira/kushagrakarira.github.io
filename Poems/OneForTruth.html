One For Truth

Falling into darkness,
Fading into chaos,
Truth all around,
As the world becomes one.

We had a purpose,
To smile and laugh,
To capture the world in our senses
To forget and to move forward

We had a dream,
We had a prayer,
The shield and the sword,
The strength and the knowledge

Grant me a wish
Unravel the truth
I seek nothing else
But a journey, never-ending

Imprisoned by my own mind
Limited by my own strength
Captured by my own lies
Stabbed by my own friend

Here comes the gate
Gate of river Styx
Follow me to truth
Pay the promised price

— Kushagra Karira
