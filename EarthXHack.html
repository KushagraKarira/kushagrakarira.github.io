University of Texas at Dallas Challenge


Challenge Description :

Due to current events, many universities have transitioned to virtual platforms to continue to teach students. How might educational institutions for all ages continue to provide the best educational opportunities possible for all students, especially those that are underserved and don’t always have access to the latest technology ?


The Interpretation :

We ourselves being students are subject to the same problem. So, we believe our Hack to be more effective and realistic.

Firstly, we tried to identify our problems and list them, so each of them could be addressed, they are :

  • Unauthorised Access by Third Party - Most of the time there are unauthorised people accessing the classroom.

  • Data Requirement - It takes a lot of bandwidth to stream all the videos and audio together consuming much data, most of the time on mobile data.

  • Lag - Since video is streaming, it lags.



The Normal communication process over other apps :

Our modification :

The Hack :

Our idea uses phones and computers to translate the audio - video in a much simpler format.

The said application is to make certain internet tests and apply the solution relatively. That is - most of the android smartphones ( 4.0 + ) use accessibility technologies called screen reader or text-to-speech and caption ( speech-to-text ) so the bandwidth requirement drops to less than 100KB/s for a class and sends over just the converted text not the audio.

and for the video part it could be easily converted to avatars !!

( So even our introvert friends can participate, who are usually shy in speaking or showing their faces on video calls )

> Think of the idea as talking to virtual people, but behind them are your classmates and the university faculty.

  • Since there’s nobody working in the university, we also propose the entire communication to be handled by the University’s Underutilized Servers for the last pack of privacy, by enabling logging in by university ID’s and automatically marking them present on the presence of 3/4th of the lecture.

Design Mockup

The design is equally important for us as the technology beneath,

As we are dealing with somebody who is just a normal person ( uses phone for calls and social media ) we have worked out an intuitive and automotive design.

The first picture, that is our home screen - shows the schedule, assignments and other details just after opening the app - and it auto connects with the class once the time matches.

The second image is an in video interface - The microphone is a push to talk one, and ass our idea - the voice is actually text that is being decoded in the phone. So the student also gets an option to turn on screen-text as well.




Environmental Problems solved :

Since servers are centralized and process a lot of information, they generate a lot of heat that contributes to global warming. Our idea uses decentralized processing by using modern protocols and client side processing.

So, our idea also helps deal with global warming in times where thousands of classes are hosted online on a single server.
